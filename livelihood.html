Livelihood and Employability

Livelihood is combination of activities undertaken by a household to ensure a living or basic needs. The choice of livelihood is based on the skills, availability or resources, gender, qualification, creativity, geography and many more. Svaja believe that livelihood should provide means for living, not at the cost of social - human and environmental values but with a sense of freedom and happiness:

• Livelihood should provide sense of freedom and pride by adding some value to the community.

• Reduce poverty and inequality with financial stability.

• Should have factor of happiness and fulfilment for people involved.

• Scope for learning innovation, and growth.

With this thought, Svaja is dedicated to enhancing access to livelihood opportunities and strengthening entrepreneur eco-system through livelihood and employability support interventions and need based livelihood programs. Bridging the gap of access to better marketplace for rural and artisan product to support local livelihood and enterprises will create value towards Atam Nirbhar Bharat .
