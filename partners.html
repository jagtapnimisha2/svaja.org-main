Partners

Svaja believes that collective efforts with commitment transform lives and communities effectively and provide opportunity to expand the boundaries for a common cause. In this journey Svaja has two types of partners:

1. Outreach partners - those who support our interventions to reach other geography for scaling up the interventions.

2. Resource partners - those who support us with financial resources to reach out to needy and underserved section of the community.
